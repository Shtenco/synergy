## Consolidation decision

- The legacy multi-threaded robot contains live order capability and must remain quarantined from funded execution until credential isolation, deterministic risk limits, kill switch and independent strategy validation pass.
- Modern MIDAS is the strongest candidate for a modular research/decision architecture, but its simulated execution adapter and test coverage do not establish a live broker service.
- MIDAS Lite contains potentially useful sealed-evaluation methodology; preserve its specialist evidence separately from the agent experiment and do not convert modeled pips into realized P&L.
- Future architecture should expose versioned signal, portfolio proposal, risk authorization and execution receipt contracts; no repository merge is required.
