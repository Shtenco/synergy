## Interpretation

A branch without an open PR is not automatically obsolete; it may be a long-running research branch, a merged/closed PR branch, or an intentionally retained baseline. Do not bulk-delete branches based on branch age, duplicate HEAD, or lack of an open PR. First inspect closed/merged PR history, unique commits relative to main, CI artifacts, tags/releases and external references.
